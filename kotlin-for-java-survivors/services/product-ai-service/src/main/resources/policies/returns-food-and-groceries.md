# Returns: food and groceries

Shelfwise has specific return rules for packaged and perishable food items.

## Packaged food

Packaged food that is unopened can be returned within 7 days of purchase with the bill. The item must not have been opened or consumed.

## Quality issues

Opened or perishable items (fresh produce, dairy, bakery) are not returnable under normal circumstances. However, if the item has a quality problem—spoiled, damaged, or past its best-before date at the time of sale—it can be replaced or refunded. Quality issues must be reported within 48 hours of purchase and you must provide the bill or a photo of the product label showing the best-before date or damage.

## Final sale items

Items marked as "final sale" or on clearance are not returnable for any reason.

## What qualifies as perishable

Perishable items include fresh vegetables, fruits, dairy products, eggs, bakery items, and similar goods that have a limited shelf life.
