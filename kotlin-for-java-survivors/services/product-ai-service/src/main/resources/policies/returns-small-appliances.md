# Returns and repairs: small appliances

Shelfwise has clear rules for small appliance returns and repairs.

## Return policy

Unused small appliances (mixer grinders, kettles, toasters, irons) can be returned within 10 days of purchase for a full refund to the original payment method. The appliance must be in the original sealed or complete packaging and the bill must be provided. Used appliances are not returnable under any circumstances.

## Warranty and repair

An appliance that stops working within the manufacturer's warranty period is repaired or replaced through the brand's authorised service centre. The warranty period is stated on the warranty card, usually 12 months; mixer grinder motors have a 24-month warranty. The store will help you register the warranty claim, but the manufacturer is responsible for the repair or replacement, not the store.

## Dead on arrival

If an appliance fails on first use and you report it within 48 hours of purchase, the store will replace it on the spot if the same model is in stock.

## No exceptions

There is no "no questions asked" return policy.
