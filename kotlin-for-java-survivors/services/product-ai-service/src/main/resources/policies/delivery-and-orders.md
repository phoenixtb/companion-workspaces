# Home delivery and online orders

Shelfwise delivers to homes near our stores under specific terms.

## Delivery areas and hours

Home delivery is available within 5 km of any Shelfwise store. Delivery hours are from 8 am to 9 pm, 7 days a week.

## Delivery charges

Delivery is free on orders of ₹499 or more. Orders below ₹499 incur a delivery charge of ₹40.

## Delivery timing

Orders placed before 6 pm are delivered the same day. Orders placed after 6 pm will be delivered the next day.

## Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel an order free of charge until it is packed for delivery. Once an order is packed, you cannot cancel it. If you refuse delivery of perishable items after the order has been packed, no refund is given. For non-perishable items refused after packing, contact the store to discuss your options.

## Order placement

Orders are placed online through the Shelfwise app or website.
